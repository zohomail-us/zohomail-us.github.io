How to get help with Zoho Mail

Identify whether the issue concerns account access, sending or receiving messages, mobile synchronization, mail-client settings, domain configuration, or billing. Recording the exact symptom helps you find the right instructions.

Prepare details before requesting assistance

Write down the complete error message, when the issue began, the device and browser or app version, and any recent changes. For delivery issues, retain the bounce message. Remove private message content and sensitive account details from screenshots.

Account and password safety

Use Zoho's genuine website and official recovery process. Never share your password, one-time verification code, recovery code, or full payment details with an unknown person. Be cautious of unexpected calls, pop-ups, and messages asking you to install remote-access software.

Email delivery and app configuration

Check sender and recipient addresses, mailbox storage, spam folders, filters, forwarding rules, and bounce notices. For IMAP, SMTP, or mobile app issues, compare settings with Zoho's current documentation. Avoid changing mail-routing DNS records until you have verified the correct values.

Billing and subscription questions

Review the plan, renewal date, invoices, and payment status in the official Zoho account portal. Use verified Zoho billing support for account-specific transaction questions. Do not send full card numbers or security codes through ordinary chat or email.
